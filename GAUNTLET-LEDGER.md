# Gauntlet ledger (how this repo was built)

The repo was built under the Gauntlet rule: the builder never judges its own work. A fresh critic (a separate agent with no access to the builder's reasoning) reviews the real files against primary sources (Grok Bot, Whop, Higgsfield, xAI, Google and Apify docs, and the motion-video-kit reference) and names the biggest gap. The builder fixes it, and a new critic verifies.
