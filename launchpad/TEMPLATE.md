# Launchpad: which AI business fits you?

**What it is:** a short interview Bot. It asks about you (what you're good at, who you already reach, your budget, your time) and recommends one of the three business templates, with a direction fitted to you. You get the install link and a first-week plan.

**What it does:** asks 6–8 questions → recommends 1 template + 2 alternatives, each tied to your answers → explains costs, time to launch and the work that stays with you → hands over the template link.

**What stays with you:** choosing. Launchpad doesn't install anything, connect any accounts or spend any money.

**Cost:** a few minutes of Grok Bot usage. No other accounts needed.

**Time:** about 10 minutes.

| Template | Fits people who… |
|---|---|
| **AI Influencer** | like internet culture, have taste in a niche and can post every day |
| **Motion Studio** | like design or software, and are fine pitching startups by email |
| **Spatial Studio** | know (or want to sell to) real estate, construction, retail or events, and like 3D |
