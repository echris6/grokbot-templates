# Pricing research (before quoting)

**The Bot never picks a price.** The user sets every price. The Bot brings evidence: what the buyer already pays for the nearest substitute, and what competitors charge. There are no numbers in this kit on purpose. The only figure, "$5k–$10k per floor plan" from the original post, is an **unverified claim** and is never quoted to a client.

## Method (about 20 minutes per idea; redo every 3 months)

1. **Substitutes the buyer pays for today.** Each idea file lists them (e.g. builders: model homes, 3D renderings, Matterport scans of built homes, virtual staging). For each one, search for public price pages:
   - `"<substitute>" pricing per <unit>`, e.g. `"3d floor plan rendering" pricing per plan`, `"interactive floor plan" price`, `"virtual tour" builder price per plan`
   - vendor pricing pages (only public ones; never sign up or log in to see prices)
2. **Freelance marketplaces** (read-only, public search pages): search Upwork and Fiverr for the deliverable (`"3d floor plan walkthrough"`, `"three.js product configurator"`, `"interactive 3d floor plan"`). Note 5 listings: price, what's included, delivery time.
3. **Agencies:** `"<deliverable>" agency "starting at"` and `"<deliverable>" "per plan"`.
4. **The buyer's own spend** (best evidence): when a prospect replies, ask what they currently pay for renderings or tours per plan. Write it down verbatim.
5. **Record:** `/workspace/spatial-studio/research/prices-<idea-slug>.md`, one row per source:

| Date | Source (URL) | What it is | Price as stated | Unit | Includes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

6. **Summarise for the user:** the low, middle and high of what you found, with links, and how our deliverable compares (scope, turnaround, interactivity). The user picks the number.

## Rules

- Quote the user's chosen price in their currency. Whop fees (`_shared/whop.md`) come off the top. Tell the user the net.
- **Per unit:** one plan, one unit type, one product, one booth. Volume pricing for "the rest of the catalog" is the user's call. Suggest a per-unit price plus a catalog price only if the user asks.
- New Whop accounts are capped at $2,500 per checkout link. Above that, check the approval or split into deposit and balance orders (Recipe B, step 4).
- Never present a competitor's price as ours, and never claim we're "cheaper than X" without the source row.
