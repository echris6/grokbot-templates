# Guide outline ("How <Character> is made")

The guide sells the **method**: how the user actually makes the videos. It states what it teaches, not what the buyer will earn (Whop requires substantiated earnings claims, and this guide makes none). Target length is 15–25 PDF pages.

## Sections

1. **What this is (1 page):** who the character is, what the guide covers, what it doesn't (no income promises), the tools and their current prices with the date.
2. **Designing a character (3 pages):** the strange-face, immaculate-clothes idea; the hard rules (fictional, adult, no look-alikes); the reference-sheet prompt; this character's real sheet as the worked example.
3. **Finding trends (3 pages):** how to find repost pages (the method, not a list of handles), the 4-day window, ranking by views and outliers, the single-take, one-person filter, and an example trend card from the user's own log with numbers taken from it.
4. **Filming your take (2 pages):** the shot list, plus common mistakes (cuts, other people in frame, logos, hands over the face).
5. **Swapping the character in (3 pages):** Genjutsu object-swap vs Gemini Omni vs Grok Imagine recreate: limits, prices and when to use which. The consent rule: only footage of yourself, or of people who signed a release, or licensed stock; never someone else's clip. The identity check.
6. **Captions and labels (2 pages):** the caption playbook, plus the AI-label steps on Instagram and TikTok.
7. **The daily routine (1 page):** a 20-minute checklist.
8. **Prompt templates (2–3 pages):** sheet, recreate, swap and caption prompts.
9. **Costs (1 page):** a table from the user's own `log.md` (real spend per video), and the date of the prices.

## Rules

- Every number comes from the user's own files (`log.md`, the job ledgers, `state/followers.csv`) or from a cited price page with a date. No invented results, testimonials or screenshots of other creators.
- No third-party clips, frames, handles or captions anywhere in the guide. Images are the character's own outputs.
- No scraping code or tool for sale (Whop prohibits selling data-scraping tools). The guide may name Apify as the service the user uses.
- Access period stated on the first page and on the product: "12 months of access, including updates" (or whatever the user picked; never "lifetime").
- The user reads the whole PDF before it goes on Whop.
